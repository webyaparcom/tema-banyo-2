Questions

Questions pinned to the board

We wrote the questions asked most at surveys on notes.

  • How long does a bathroom renovation take?

    A 5–6 m² bathroom usually takes 10–14 working days including demolition; the two-day waterproofing test is included.

  • Can you work while we live at home?

    Yes. We put up dust screens and sweep and close the area every evening; for single-bathroom homes we discuss a temporary toilet at the survey.

  • How do you test the waterproofing?

    After waterproofing the floor is flooded for 48 hours and checked from the flat below before tiling starts. You receive the test photos.

  • Can we choose the tiles ourselves?

    Of course. The workshop has a sample wall; we can also lay material you buy yourself — we prepare the quantity list.

  • Is the survey charged?

    Surveys are free in Karsiyaka, Bayrakli and Bornova; for other districts travel is listed separately in the quote.

  • I rent — do I need the landlord’s approval?

    For plumbing and finish changes we ask for written approval; we prepare a job summary you can send to the landlord.
