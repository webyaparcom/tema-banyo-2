Method

Wet room

Four layers under the tiles

The hidden layers decide how long a bathroom lasts; each one is photographed for the handover file.

  1. tile_large
    01

    Tiles and grout

    Non-slip floor tiles; epoxy or cement grout, colour chosen at the sample wall.

  2. layers
    02

    Flexible adhesive

    Wet-area adhesive applied to the full surface, leaving no voids.

  3. water_drop
    03

    Waterproofing

    Two coats of liquid membrane with mesh tape at the corners; up to 2 metres on the shower wall.

  4. foundation
    04

    Screed and fall

    A screed with a 1–2% fall towards the drain, so no water pools.
